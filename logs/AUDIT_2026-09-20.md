## 2. Errors in the data and the scripts

1. **Asturias weighting bug** (`07_exam_coding.py`). `points / n_options` is right for
   "choose 1 of n" slots and wrong for "answer any 5 of 8": Asturias's expected-exposure
   weights summed to 2.0 instead of 10, so its contextualised share read 10 % / 12.5 %
   instead of 50 % / 62.5 %. Correlation of Δgrade with Δcontextualised points: **−0.887 → −0.852**.
   Figure 12's Asturias stack was drawn at a fifth of its height. Fixed by normalising each
   paper's weights to its total marks (a no-op for the other seventeen papers).
2. **Two theory questions missing** (`08_ehu_history.py`). July 2019 option B carries
   *Cámara fotográfica* (title 6) and *Campos de fuerza conservativos…* (title 10); only
   option A was recorded. **118 → 120 questions**; the camera was set twice, not once; the
   five most frequent titles account for 47 of 120, and the five the chapter named were not
   the five most frequent (Coulomb, 8, outranks simple harmonic motion, 7).
3. **Silent merge failure** (`18_reading_load.py`). Short vs long community names dropped
   Asturias and Madrid, so the length–competency collinearity was computed on six papers:
   **+0.898 (n=6) → +0.788 (n=8, p = 0.020)**.
4. **Centring artefact** (`14_take_up.py` / `13_cohort_drift.py`). Both variables were
   centred within community over all eleven years and the test then run on the six
   non-plateau ones, leaving the plotted cloud 0.3 below a zero line labelled "relative to
   the community's own average". Centred on the years used: **r = −0.096, p = 0.339**
   (reported: −0.020, p = 0.839). Conclusion unchanged.
5. **Missing p-values.** `exam_correlations.json` carried r without p, and both Ch. 11 and
   Ch. 14 asserted that no correlation was significant. The contextualised-points one is:
   **r = −0.852, p = 0.0035**.

## 6. The decomposition — the study's new result

`scripts/21_decay_decomposition.py`, figure 25, `data/analysis/decomposition*.{json,csv}`.

**Typology.** Each Basque transition classified as a change of location or of shape, against
the locus fitted on all 187 region-years:

- **2023 → 2024**: Δmean −0.21, top band −9.0 pp against a locus prediction of −3.1
  (**excess −5.9**), pass rate **+2.3** against a prediction of −2.8 (**excess +5.0**).
  A compression. As a move of the conditional residual it is **−3.08 SD, rank 2 of the 170
  transitions** in the panel (Cantabria 2025 is −3.98) and the largest outside the LOMLOE year.
- **2024 → 2025**: Δmean −0.62 with both band excesses under 1.8 pp. A pure level shift.
- **2025 → 2026**: unclassifiable — EHU has not published the distribution. The two 2026
  cohorts that do publish one (ULL, ULPGC) are pure level shifts.

**Mark budget** against the nine communities with a 2026 result, pooled phase:
2024 Basque-specific −0.18; 2025 **+0.03** (the whole of that year's fall was national);
2026 **−1.65**.

**Attribution of the −1.65**: choice removed −0.26 (range −0.14 to −0.36 over item SD
0.1–0.3); cohort −0.18 (±0.11); **quantified −0.44, unattributed −1.21 = 73 %**. The four
candidates for the remainder — paper content, reading load, marking severity, syllabus
exposure — cannot be separated from one another in a mean. The cross-community
length–grade slope, taken literally, would assign −2.27 marks to the Basque paper's +45.3 %,
more than the whole Basque-specific fall: a reason not to take it literally.

## 7. What this changes

- The first audit's retraction of the "2024 Basque signal" was right about the *level* and
  wrong to stop there: nobody had tested the *transition*, which is what "a turn" means.
- Every mean-based test in the study is blind to 2024, and all of them are correct. The first
  thing that changed in the Basque exam changed the top of the distribution, on the last
  pre-LOMLOE paper.
- The data requests are now ordered by which component each would separate, and sub-task
  marks — not the tribunal table — is first, because it separates all four unattributed
  components at once.
- Chapters 13 and 14 (and the Spanish briefing) were rewritten around this.
