## 3. Findings, ranked by consequence

### 3.1 Interpretive claims withdrawn (Chapter 6)

1. **"The plateau reshaped the distribution."** The conditional top-band ratio
   P(x ≥ 8 | x ≥ 5) rose 0.353 → 0.466 over 2020–24, but it tracks the mean at
   r = 0.905; the quadratic locus predicts 0.356 → 0.442 from the level alone. Residual
   excess +0.021, p = 0.17 on year means (5 v 5), carried by 2020. The plateau was a lift,
   not a reshaping. *Rewritten around the residual; fig 19 rebuilt (panel b = residual)
   and included as `#fig-condtop`.*
2. **"A Basque signal that arrives a year early."** The claim of "eight consecutive
   years on the other side" was false (2015, 2020, 2021 negative). The 2024–25 two-year
   negative turn of the residual (< −0.5 SD against the field) is shared by Aragón,
   Castilla y León, Comunitat Valenciana and La Rioja — 5 of 17; 9 of 17 have some two-year
   run; 3 of 17 have ≥ 7 positive years 2016–23 (Euskadi has 6). Not a Basque signal.
   *Section rewritten around the base rate (`data/audit/conditional_turn_base_rate.csv`).*
3. **"The two instruments fall at the same rate (1.72× vs 1.66×, within 4 %)."** Stale
   (§2) and ill-posed: both records are steps (PISA 2015 and 2025; PAU plateau-end and
   2026), not slopes; the four-round ratio compares slopes with p = 0.19 and 0.38.
   *Window pinned to 2012–22 with the reason stated; five-round result reported; conclusion
   rewritten as steps-not-slopes.*

## 4. Gaps: tests the chapter should have run, now run

`scripts/19_audit_checks.py` → `data/audit/*.csv`, `data/tables/audit_*.md`:

1. **Plateau by subject** (17 communities, each subject vs its own 2015–19 norm). The
   2020–24 plateau is present in every subject (Matemáticas II +0.5 to +1.0, Química +0.6
   to +0.9, Biología +0.2 to +0.6, Historia +0.3 to +0.8, Lengua +0.2 to +0.5), and in
   2025 every science subject returned to within ±0.2 of its norm while the humanities kept
   part of the lift. Strengthens the reference-frame argument and keeps its confound
   explicit: the 2025 return to norm is subject-general (so not a Física effect of the
   competency model) but coincides with the first LOMLOE year.
2. **Base rate of the conditional turn** — see §3.1(2).
3. **Euskadi's subjects vs their own norms**: in 2025 Física −0.39 is the only Basque
   science subject clearly below its norm (Química −0.01, Matemáticas II +0.27, Biología
   +0.31). The cleanest within-Basque comparison the data allow, and it points at the
   Física paper from 2025.
4. **2026 vs pre-COVID norm, nine communities**: the "+0.02" nine-community average
   hides a two-point spread (Madrid +1.02 … País Vasco −1.91).

Sub-questions identified but not answerable with the data in hand: item-level marks of
