**Instructions.** 4 apartados. A: 2 pts, no choice. B and C: 3 pts each = one fixed question of 0.5 pts + one of two questions of 2.5 pts. D: 2 pts, choose one of two questions. Spelling penalty: −0.25 per 3 faults, max 1 pt.

**Apartado A (2 pts, obligatory).** Context: "El 1 abril de 2026 se lanzó la misión Artemis II, que transportaba a la nave Orion con cuatro astronautas para la exploración de la cara oculta de la Luna. Uno de los aspectos clave para garantizar la seguridad de la tripulación es la reentrada en la atmósfera terrestre y el aterrizaje …" Orion (m = 26.3·10³ kg) in a circular orbit at h = 100 km.
- A1 (1 pt): calculate the orbital velocity and compare it with the escape velocity.
- A2 (1 pt): before re-entry the service module separates and only the capsule (m₀ = 9.3·10³ kg) descends; calculate the total mechanical energy the capsule would have at rest on the surface and determine the energy to dissipate (mainly as heat) for a safe, smooth landing. Data given.

**Apartado B (3 pts).** B1 (0.5 pt, fixed): work done to move a 4 nC charge from V₁ = 200 V to V₂ = 500 V; reason the result. Choose B2.A or B2.B (2.5 pts):
- B2.A: rectangular loop (5 × 10 cm) rotating at ω = 2π rad/s in B = 10 T, R = 5 Ω; (a) deduce flux vs time and evaluate at t = 0.5 s; (b) induced emf and current vs time, maximum value. Figure.
- B2.B: two charged conducting spheres q₁ = q₂ = 10 µC, radii 10 cm and 5 cm; sphere 2 constrained to vertical motion under gravity, m₂ = 0.1 kg (schematic figure); (a) force diagram and equilibrium distance; (b) connect the spheres with an ideal wire, compute charge and potential of each after connection.

**Apartado C (3 pts).** C1 (0.5 pt, fixed): construct the image of an object between f and 2f of a converging lens and describe its characteristics. Choose C2.A or C2.B (2.5 pts):
- C2.A: MAS x(t) = 10 cos(πt/2 + π) cm, m = 0.1 kg; (a) spring constant, kinetic energy and velocity at the midpoint; (b) equation of position when the midpoint velocity is 20π cm/s.
- C2.B: electromagnetic wave, λ = 10 nm, f = 2.5·10¹⁶ Hz, E₀ = 50 V/m; (a) angular frequency, wave number, wave equation; (b) propagation velocity and refractive index of the medium.

**Apartado D (2 pts, choose one).**
- D1: alpha particles vs protons with the same kinetic energy (non-relativistic); (a) velocity ratio; (b) de Broglie wavelength ratio.
- D2: ¹⁸F radiotracer in PET/TAC, T½ = 110 min, initial 1.5·10⁻¹² g; (a) decay constant; (b) remaining mass after 5.5 h.
