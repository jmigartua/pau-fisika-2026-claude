# Castilla-La Mancha — PAU Física, convocatoria ordinaria 2025 (curso 2024/2025)

Source: https://www.uclm.es/-/media/Files/A04-Gestion-Academica/PDFEstudiantes/PDFEvAU/ModelosPruebas2425/FISICA.ashx?la=es (2 pages; read in the built-in browser on 19 Sep 2026; transcribed from the rendered pages, abridged).

**Instructions.** 4 questions of 2.5 pts: three problems and one set of "cuestiones". Each problem has three sections a/b/c of 1.25 pts; the candidate chooses two. Cuestiones: choose two of three (1.25 pts each).

**Problema 1 (2.5 pts, choose 2 of 3).** Spacecraft of mass 9·10⁴ kg on the surface of Saturn (R = 5.82·10⁴ km, M = 5.68·10²⁶ kg, rotation period 10 h 34 min). (a) surface gravity and escape velocity, deduce the expressions; (b) launched vertically, engines off at 2 R with v = 1 km/s — maximum height reached; (c) height of a synchronous ("geoestacionaria") orbit. No narrative context beyond the setting.

**Problema 2 (2.5 pts, choose 2 of 3).** Mass spectrometer: two particles of equal mass m = 5·10⁻⁶ kg enter a region with B = 0.50 T (figure), same speed 100 m/s, semicircular trajectories with x₁ = 10 cm and x₂ = 40 cm. (a) reason the sign of each charge and determine the charges; (b) acceleration due to the magnetic force and time to complete the semicircle; (c) electric field to add so that the particles go straight (velocity selector). Figure.

**Problema 3 (2.5 pts, choose 2 of 3).** Object 10 cm tall, 5 cm to the left of a diverging lens of −10 D. (a) ray tracing and image characteristics; (b) numerical position, size and magnification; (c) replace by a converging lens of the same power; ray tracing and real/virtual reasoning.

**Cuestiones (2.5 pts, choose 2 of 3).** (a) photoelectric effect: aluminium W = 4.08 eV, threshold frequency and kinetic energy under 250 nm light; (b) two conducting spheres (R₁ = 90 cm, R₂ = 45 cm) at V₁ = 10 V and V₂ = 20 V, charge after connecting them; (c) school-laboratory pendulum experiment to determine g (figure): c.1 different bob mass — same result? why?; c.2 length needed to double the period; c.3 period on the Moon (g six times smaller) if 2 s on Earth.

Structure summary: 4 questions; 10 pts; every question has internal choice (2 of 3 sections) → optionality on 100 % of the paper. Contextualised items: Cuestión c (laboratory pendulum, experimental reasoning). Problem 1 and 2 are classic settings (Saturn, mass spectrometer) with "deduce razonadamente" wording; no real-world narrative, no judgment/evaluation section.
